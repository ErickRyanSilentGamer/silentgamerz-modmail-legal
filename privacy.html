SilentGamerz Modmail Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SilentGamerz Modmail ("the Service") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when users interact with the Service through Discord.

1. Information We Process

SilentGamerz Modmail may process information necessary to operate the Service, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server information
  • User roles
  • Messages submitted through Modmail
  • Modmail ticket and thread identifiers
  • Ticket status and related information
  • Blacklist records
  • Relevant moderation and staff actions

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by SilentGamerz Modmail may be used to:

  • Operate the Modmail service
  • Deliver messages between users and authorized staff
  • Create and manage Modmail tickets
  • Provide support to users
  • Enforce moderation actions and blacklists
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or misuse of the Service
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service

3. Access to Information

Authorized SilentGamerz staff may access Modmail conversations and related ticket information when necessary to provide support, perform moderation duties, investigate abuse, or maintain the Service.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Certain information may be stored while necessary to operate SilentGamerz Modmail, maintain tickets, enforce moderation actions, prevent abuse, or maintain appropriate service records.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted in accordance with the Service's operational and moderation requirements.

5. Data Sharing

SilentGamerz does not sell personal information collected through SilentGamerz Modmail.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate or host SilentGamerz Modmail where necessary for providing the Service.

6. Data Requests and Deletion

Users may contact the SilentGamerz team to ask questions about applicable information associated with them or request deletion where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, moderation, or legal obligations.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by SilentGamerz Modmail. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Discord

SilentGamerz Modmail operates through Discord. Users are also subject to Discord's applicable terms, policies, and requirements when using Discord.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SilentGamerz Modmail, its features, or its data practices change. The "Last Updated" date will be changed when the policy is updated.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official SilentGamerz support system.
